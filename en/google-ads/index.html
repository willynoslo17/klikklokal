Google Ads for local businesses

Google Ads are paid search ads that appear when someone in your area searches for what you sell. You pay for clicks – not just impressions.

This I do

  • Setting up an account in your name
  • Keywords and negative keywords
  • Advertising texts in Norwegian and/or other languages
  • Geographic targeting
  • Call and form conversion tracking
  • Link to Google Business profile
  • Weekly control and monthly optimization
  • Monthly report

Conversion tracking

The tracking is installed on your website and is only activated with consent to cookies (banner). If you don't have a banner today, I will arrange it in the offer.

Common questions

How long will it take before I see results?

It depends on the industry, competition and budget. The first weeks are often spent learning and adjusting the campaign.

What if I already have a Google Ads account?

I can go through it and use it further. The account remains yours.

Can I pause the ads?

Yes. The advertising budget can be paused in the platform. The fixed monthly price follows the conditions for termination.
