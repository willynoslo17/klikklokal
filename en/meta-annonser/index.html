Meta ads for local businesses

Facebook ads via Meta help people in the local area get to know your company, an offer or an event - even before they actively search.

what do i do

  • Target group in the local area
  • Advertising texts
  • Simple ads created from your photos and videos
  • Testing 2–3 variants
  • Optimization and monthly report

I gain access via partner roles in the Meta Business Suite - never by sharing passwords.

Material

The ads are created from the images and videos you have. If you need new videos, this can be agreed separately.

Honesty rule

I don't use fake reviews and I don't present AI generated people as customers. The ads follow Meta's advertising policies.

Price: Meta Local

All prices are ex. VAT The advertising budget comes in addition and is paid by you directly to Google or Meta.

NOK 2,990per month e.g. VAT · no start-up cost

No binding period - 1 month's notice. You own the ad account. No percent of the advertising budget.
