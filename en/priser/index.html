Common questions

Why don't you take a percentage of the advertising budget?

Because you should be able to increase your budget without my price automatically increasing. You pay a fixed monthly price for my work and the budget goes directly to Google or Meta.

What happens to the account if the partnership ends?

The account is yours all the way, with history and settings. You retain access.

Do I need a website?

A website helps a lot for tracking and trust. If you don't have it, I'll look at alternatives in the free check.

Can I advertise in Spanish?

Yes. I can create advertisements in Norwegian, Spanish and English - useful if your customers speak several languages.

How is the report?

You get a monthly report in simple language: what it cost, and what you got (clicks, calls, inquiries) as measured by the platforms.

How much should I spend on ads?

How much you should spend on ads depends on the industry and area. I will go through that with you in the free check.
